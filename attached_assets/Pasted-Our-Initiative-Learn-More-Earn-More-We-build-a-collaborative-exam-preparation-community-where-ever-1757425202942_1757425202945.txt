Our Initiative: Learn More, Earn More We build a collaborative exam-preparation community where every contribution matters and everyone benefits. How it works 1. Upload questions & solutions Students upload questions or their solutions for community review. Reviewed and accepted submissions become part of the shared learning pool. Accepted contributions earn credits, stars, or other community benefits. 2. Review & improve together Members can submit alternate solutions to existing problems. Accepted solutions receive recognition and rewards. Multiple solutions provide varied approaches and deepen understanding for everyone. 3. Star system & community benefits A star/points system recognizes contributions and quality. Increased participation unlocks benefits such as access to top-rated content, peer support, and community recognition. This is not monetary—value comes from collaboration and shared learning. Enhancing the star system To make recognition meaningful, the star system functions as a reputation framework that rewards effort and quality. Levels & badges Stars accumulate toward levels (Beginner → Explorer → Mentor → Master). Badges mark specific achievements like Top Reviewer, Problem Creator, or Community Helper. Leaderboards Weekly and monthly leaderboards highlight top contributors. Separate boards for categories like most questions uploaded, most accepted solutions, and most helpful reviews. Collaboration boosts Stars unlock benefits such as: Access to premium-quality questions Entry to exclusive discussion groups Early access to new problem sets Peer recognition Community upvotes surface the most helpful solutions. Highly rated answers earn extra stars and visibility. Contribution milestones Rewards for key milestones (e.g., first 10 reviews, first 50 stars, first accepted solution). Milestone rewards could include advanced study tips, curated problem sets, or mentor recognition. Community challenges Topic-focused or time-bound events (e.g., “Algebra Week”, “Past Paper Sprint”). Completing challenges awards bonus stars and recognition. Helper roles Stars enable community roles: Reviewer (validate submissions), Mentor (guide peers), Curator (organize topics). Roles foster ownership and improve content quality. Why this works Stars and reputation: Recognize meaningful contribution, not just activity. Encourage consistent participation and improvement. Create visible progress and a sense of belonging. Make exam preparation more collaborative and productive. Our vision Do more, get more. Share knowledge, earn recognition, and help build a community that’s richer, smarter, and more supportive. This is a student-driven, non-paid initiative where knowledge grows through collaboration.

PM announces flood-relief aid for Punjab, Himachal
₹1,500 crore for Himachal and ₹1,600 crore for Punjab; Modi does an aerial survey of affected areas and holds a review; political parties in Punjab term the financial assistance ‘inadequate’
The Hindu Bureau
CHANDIGARH
Mr. Modi visited the two States ravaged by floods following unprecedented rain and announced relief packages.
For Punjab, the announcement is in addition to the ₹12,000 crore already in the “State’s kitty”, said a Union government statement without elaborating further.
‘Relax norms’
On Monday, Punjab Water Resources Minister Barinder Kumar Goyal said the State had more than ₹12,000 crore in the disaster management fund, and urged the Centre to relax norms to utilise the funds.
The Prime Minister did an aerial survey of the disaster-affected areas. Later, he held meetings to review the relief and rehabilitation measures. In Himachal Pradesh, the meeting was held in Kangra, while in Punjab, it was organised in Gurdaspur.
He asked for a “multi-dimensional view to be taken to get the entire region and people back on their feet”.
“These would be done through multiple ways, like rebuilding homes through PM Awas Yojana, restoration of National highways, rebuilding schools, provision of relief under PM National Relief Fund (PMNRF), along with release of mini kits for livestock,” the statement said.
Recognising the need to support farmers, the government will provide additional assistance to those who lack power connections, it added.
In Himachal, under the PM Awas Yojana, damaged houses will be geotagged to help in accurate assessment and faster delivery of aid. In Punjab, financial assistance from the PM Awaas Yojana-Gramin will be extended under a “special project” submitted by the State government for the reconstruction of houses damaged by floods in rural areas.
Mr. Modi announced an ex gratia of ₹2 lakh to the next of kin of the deceased and ₹50,000 to those seriously injured in the calamity in both the States.
Political parties in Punjab termed the financial assistance “inadequate”.
“I urge Prime Minister Narendra Modi to enhance the financial assistance of ₹1,600 crore announced for Punjab, besides considering a one-time loan waiver for farmers in flood-affected areas,” Sukhbir Badal, president of the Shiromani Akali Dal, said.
He hit out at the ruling Aam Aadmi Party (AAP). “I demand an explanation from the AAP government after the shocking revelation during the PM’s visit that despite having ₹12,000 crore with it under the disaster management fund, it failed to release aid when it was most needed. The AAP government has compounded the woes of the Punjabis with this criminal negligence,” Mr. Badal said.
Punjab Congress president Amarinder Singh Raja Warring termed the ₹1,600-crore flood relief package for Punjab as “miserably meagre”, akin to “a drop in the ocean”.

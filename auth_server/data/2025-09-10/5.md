Police detain BJP activists, foil Chamundi Chalo rally
Rally was in protest against govt. decision to invite writer Banu Mushtaq to inaugurate Dasara festival on Chamundi Hills; BJP leaders say police are acting under pressure from government
The Hindu Bureau
MYSURU
The rally was called by the Hindu Jagaran Vedike in protest against the Siddaramaiah government’s decision to invite writer and Booker Prize winner Banu Mushtaq to inaugurate the 10-day Dasara festival on Chamundi Hills on September 22. The BJP and other right-wing groups have criticised the invite to the writer over her “anti-Kannada” stance.
The police had already denied permission for the rally citing law and order concerns and Dasara-related works under way on the hills. However, the activists assembled in large numbers at the Kurubarahalli Circle, from where the road leads to Chamundi Hills. A strong posse of police was deployed there to stop them. Multiple layers of security cordon were in place, which the BJP leaders, including former MLA L. Nagendra, described as “police atrocity”.
“Deputy Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar has already said that Chamundi Hills or Chamundeshwari temple does not belong to the Hindus alone and this has hurt our sentiments. Hence, we are making an attempt to assert our claims over the hills and the temple. Yet, the police, under duress from the government, are stopping us,” said Mr. Nagendra.
Harmony rally barred
Krishnaraja MLA T.S. Srivatsa wanted to know the grounds of preventive custody when no prohibitory orders (barring assembly) were in place.
Another large group of activists also tried to break through the police cordon, but were detained.
A ‘harmony rally’ to the hills planned by another group too was not allowed.

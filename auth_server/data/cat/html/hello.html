Q 1
Reattempt

How many distinct natural numbers 'n' are there such that, amongst all its divisors, greater than 1 and less than 'n', the largest divisor is 21 times the smallest divisor?

Solution

21 divides the number n.
So, the smallest divisor of n is either 2 or 3 (as 3 divides 21)
So, the largest divisor of n is either 21*2 = 42 or 21*3 = 63
Hence, n = 84 (21*2 = 42) and n = 189 (21*3=63)
So, number of values of n is 2.

Q 5
Reattempt

Satya, Ramesh, Harish, and Ganesh won a total of Rs. 50000 prize money in a cricket tournament. Initially, each one of them got 15% of the total prize money. Then, barring Ganesh, the other three - Satya, Ramesh, and Harish received 10%, 20% and 30% of the remaining prize money in that order. Thereafter, Satya and Harish divided the remaining prize money between them in 5 : 3 ratio in that order. What is the ratio of prize money received by Ramesh to Harish?

Solution

Prize money received by each of Satya, Ramesh, Harish, and Ganesh in round 1 = 0.15*50000 = Rs. 7500
Prize money left after round 1 = 50000 - 4*7500 = Rs. 20000
Therefore, we can say that the prize money received by Satya, Ramesh, and Harish in round 2 is Rs. 2000, Rs. 4000 and Rs. 6000 respectively.
Prize money left after round 2 = 20000 - 2000+4000+6000 = Rs. 8000. This amount is distributed by Satish and Harish in 5 : 3 ratio. Hence, Satish and Harish would have received Rs. 5000 and Rs. 3000 in round 3.
Total prize money received by Ramesh = 7500 + 4000 = Rs. 11500
Total prize money received by Harish = 7500 + 6000 + 3000 = Rs. 16500
Therefore, the required ratio = 115 : 165 or 23 : 33. Hence, option C is the correct answer.

Mayank Khandelwal Faculty

Hello, 
You went wrong at the step where you multiplied through by the denominators, such as (y² - 25) = (y - 5)(y + 5) and (5y - y²) = -y(y - 5), and treated them as if they were always positive. These expressions can be positive or negative depending on the value of y. When you multiply or cross-multiply by an expression that can change sign, the direction of the inequality can flip when that expression is negative. By ignoring that, your solution missed some valid ranges. The correct way is to combine everything under one denominator, which gives (3y + 73)/((y - 5)(y + 5)) < 0, and then perform a sign analysis using the critical points y = -73/3, -5, and 5 (excluding y = -5, 0, 5 since they make the denominator zero). This gives the solution y < -73/3 or -5 < y < 5. So, the largest integer value of y that satisfies the inequality is 4. In short, the mistake was cross-multiplying by variable expressions without considering their sign and domain restrictions.
Hope this helps, feel free to ask if you have any other queries.

Know more about asking doubts

1) Who will clear my doubts and how much time will it take?
Doubts are cleared by our expert faculty. We try to clear all doubts within 48 hours, but sometimes it takes up to a week to clear the doubts.

2) What can I do to get my doubts solved quickly?
We are having a heavy inflow of doubts. So while solving good doubts take precedence over other doubts.

3) What is considered a good doubt?
We expect the student to put in the effort to understand our solutions and ask doubts which are specific in nature.
For example, ‘In the second line, why did you assume that X > 24’ is a good doubt which we can help clear.
On the other hand,‘Did not understand the solution, please elaborate’ is a bad doubt because it appears that the student did not put in the effort to understand the solution. In case you didn't understand a solution, put in your method of solving and tell us where you got stuck.

4) How many doubts can I ask?
As of now, there is no restriction in the number of doubts a student can ask. But we expect our students to be mature and ask doubts only when needed.
A student who asks too many generic questions will be given lesser priority than a student who asks good questions.
Impolite language is not condoned and repeat offenders might be banned from asking doubts.

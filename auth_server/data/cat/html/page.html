Reattempt

Which of the following has the highest number of poor people?

Solution

Representing the data in tabular form and denoting the country with the highest population as rank 1 and the lowest as rank 2 and so on.

Since two bubbles are almost similar in size, their rank can be either 2 or 3.

Calculating the HDI based on the given data and tabulating it, we get:

It is given that A has the highest HDI. Hence country with population rank 6 is A.

Similarly, from 1, B will be the country with HDI 1.67 (Population rank is either 2 or 3). 

Condition 3: From the chart, we can reject the possibility of countries with population ranks 7 and 4 because both have the same number of literate people per 100 but there is a difference in poor people per 100 because the centres do not coincide.

From 2, population C>D>E>F

So, C has population rank 1, D has population rank 2 or 3(HDI 5). E has population rank 4 and F has population rank 7.

Hence, the new table can be formed as: 

 The population of poor people for A  = 10a   (Where 100a is the total population of country A)
The population of poor people for D = 13d     (Where 100d is the total population of country D)
The population of poor people for C = 20c     (Where 100c is the total population of country C)
The population of poor people for F = 25f      (Where 100f is the total population of country F)
Assuming 100a = 150x  => a=1.5x  (Consider 10 units of vertical axis represents 100x population)
Then, 100d = 225x  => d=2.25x
=> 100c = 250x  => c=2.5x
=> 100f = 100x  => f=x
Then, 10a = 15x, 13d = 29.25x, 20c = 50x and 25f = 25x
Hence C has the highest poor population.

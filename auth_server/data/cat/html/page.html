Q 1

A, B, C and D are points on a circle such that ABC is a equilateral triangle and AD is a diameter of the circle. If the radius of the circle is 2 cm, what is the area of the quadrilateral ABDC?

Solution

Let the side of the equilateral triangle be 'a'.
The circle shown is the circumcircle of the equilateral triangle.
The circumradius is given as 2.
wkt circumradius of an equilateral triangle is given by a3\frac{a}{\sqrt{3}}3​a​
So, a3=2\frac{a}{\sqrt{3}}=23​a​=2 => a=23a=2\sqrt{3}a=23​
The length of each side of the triangle is 232\sqrt{3}23​ cm.

For any equilateral triangle, the circumradius, median, altitude, inradius, all of them coicide
Hence, diameter AD bisects BC at E.
Hence BE = EC = 232=3\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{2}=\sqrt{3}223​​=3​
So, area of the right angled triangle ABD is 12⋅AD⋅BE=23\frac{1}{2}\cdot\text{AD}\cdot\text{BE}=2\sqrt{3}21​⋅AD⋅BE=23​ sq cm
and the area of the quadrilateral ABDC is 434\sqrt{3}43​ sq cm

OR 

Triangle ABD is a right triangle(Since AD is diameter)
Using Pythagoras theorem,  AB2+BD2=AD2\text{AB}^2+\text{BD}^2=\text{AD}^2AB2+BD2=AD2,
(23)2+BD2=42(2\sqrt{3})^2+\text{BD}^2=4^2(23​)2+BD2=42 we get BD = 2 cm.
So, length of BD and DC is 2 cm.
So, area of the right angled triangle ABD is 232\sqrt{3}23​ sq cm and the area of the quadrilateral ABDC is 434\sqrt{3}43​ sq cm

Aranya and Sharanya are standing on two ends of a diameter and the length of the chord connecting Aranya and Charu is 3\sqrt{3}3​ times the length of the chord connecting Charu and Sharanya. It is also known that the length of the chord connecting Charu and Sharanya when measured in metres is a natural number.

The distance between Aranya- Saranya is maximum, so they must be standing exactly opposite to each other i.e the diameter of the circle.

=> AS = 2xAS\ =\ 2xAS = 2x

When sides are in the ratio 1 :  3\sqrt{\ 3} 3​ : 2, then the corresponding angles would be 30∘  , 60∘ , 90∘ 30^{\circ\ \ },\ 60^{\circ\ },\ 90^{\circ\ }30∘  , 60∘ , 90∘ 

=> ∠ CAS = 30∘ \angle\ CAS\ =\ 30^{\circ\ }∠ CAS = 30∘ 

So the angle between the line joining Aranya-Saranya and Aranya-Charu is 30∘ 30^{\circ\ }30∘ 

Radius of the circle = x.

Length of the track = 2π x2\pi\ x2π x

Option A :2π x2\pi\ x2π x = 11π \pi\ π  => x = 5.5

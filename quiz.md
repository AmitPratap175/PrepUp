# Polity Quiz

## Question 1
Under the Regulating Act of $1773$, which specific structural change was made regarding the administration of the Bombay and Madras presidencies?

- [ ] The Governors of Bombay and Madras were granted independent legislative powers for their respective territories.
- [x] The presidencies were made subordinate to the Governor-General of Bengal.
- [ ] The presidencies were merged into a single administrative unit governed directly from Calcutta.
- [ ] The Governors of these presidencies were appointed members of the Governor-General’s Executive Council.

**Hint:** Consider the initial steps taken by the British to create a centralized hierarchy among the different company-held regions.

## Question 3

- [ ] It was completely abolished for all commodities and regions.

- [ ] It was restricted solely to the trade of opium and salt within the Bengal presidency.

**Hint:** Recall the two major exceptions that allowed the Company to retain a partial commercial advantage until $1833$.

## Question 4
The Charter Act of $1833$ is considered the final step towards centralization in British India. How did it alter the legislative authority of the Governors of Bombay and Madras?

- [ ] It empowered them to create regulations that could only be vetoed by the British Parliament.
- [x] It deprived them of their legislative powers, vesting exclusive authority in the Governor-General of India.
- [ ] It required them to submit all proposed laws to the Supreme Court at Calcutta for judicial review.
- [ ] It granted them the power to legislate on local matters while the Governor-General handled 'Federal' matters.

**Hint:** Focus on the shift in power that occurred when the Governor-General of Bengal became the Governor-General of India.

## Question 5

- [x] The inclusion of local representatives from provincial governments in the Central Legislative Council.

## Question 6

## Question 7

**Hint:** Think about how this act reversed the legal restrictions placed on the subordinate presidencies in $1833$.
